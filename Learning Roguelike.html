    Rules of the run

    1. Roll the d4. The number picks your task.
    2. Roll the d6. 1 is 15 minutes, 2 is 20, and so on up to 6 for 40. The blocks are short on purpose, so you stop while you still want more.
    3. Read where you left off, then start the timer.
    4. When time is up, stop. Finish the line you are on and do not start anything new.
    5. Write the cliffhanger. One sentence on the next step. It is required to bank the mark, and it is the first thing you will see the next time this task comes up.
    6. One mark per session, whatever its length. Marks take each task deeper. Touch all four tasks to clear a round and earn a reroll token. A token lets you roll the d4 or d6 again before you start.
